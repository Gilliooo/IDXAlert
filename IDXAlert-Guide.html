IDXAlert

Alerts you when IDX publishes a disclosure from a company you cover.

1Find it in the tray

Click the chevron beside the clock. IDXAlert is the green bar chart.

2Right-click the icon

Verify feed once. It should say it is reading the newest page. Then open Options.

3What the icon means

Options, Filters tab. Type your tickers, tick four boxes, Save.

Then the Schedule tab, tick Start automatically when I log in. Settings save instantly, no restart.

When it fires
Left-click a rowopens the PDF. The window stays open.
Right-click or ✕closes it. It never closes by itself.
Several filesclick to pick one, or Open all.
If it goes wrong
No alertsFilters too tight. Empty both boxes, confirm alerts start, then add back one at a time.
Too manyTick the four boxes. Narrow your ticker list.
Red iconUsually the VPN. Try it off.
Double alertsTwo copies running. Quit both, start one.
The one thing to know. The time on each row is when the company filed, not when you were told. IDX publishes in batches on the hour and half hour, about 18 minutes after filing. IDXAlert reaches you under a minute after that. A row showing 19:13 at 19:30 is working correctly.
